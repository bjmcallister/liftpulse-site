Common questions

How do I change my equipment?

Tap the gym name at the top of the Today, Train or Exercises screen, then tap the pencil next to it. Tick what you have. The exercise list and your program update straight away. To add another place, such as an office gym, tap Add a place.

Why did my program change an exercise?

Programs are written with a first-choice exercise for each slot. If your gym doesn't have the equipment for it, LiftPulse swaps in the closest exercise you can do, like a goblet squat instead of a barbell squat. Add the equipment and the first choice comes back.

How does LiftPulse pick my weights?

The first time, it suggests a light starting weight based on your experience. After that it shows what you did last time. When you complete every rep of every set, it suggests the next small step up. You can always tap a weight or rep count to change it.

Can I change the demo figure?

Yes. Settings (the person icon on Today) > About you lets you pick male, female or prefer not to say.

Can I turn the voice off?

Settings > Voice coach.

Is my data private?

Yes. There's no account and no server: everything stays on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.

Is LiftPulse medical advice?

No. It gives general fitness guidance. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise program, and stop if anything hurts. See the terms of use.

LiftPulse

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

The short version: LiftPulse does not collect your data. There is no account, no server, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you log stays on your iPhone.

This policy covers the LiftPulse app for iPhone, made by Billy McAllister ("I", "me"). It explains what the app keeps, where it keeps it, and the choices you have.

What LiftPulse keeps on your device

Everything you enter is saved only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • about you: the demo figure you chose (male, female or prefer not to say), and, if you enter them, your age, height and body weight
  • your experience level, goal and training days
  • the places you train and the equipment each one has
  • your program, workouts, sets, weights and reps
  • your settings

None of it is sent to me or to anyone else. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, Apple includes the app's data in that device backup, under Apple's terms.

Why LiftPulse asks about you

Your answers choose which demo figure you see and set sensible starting weights. They're optional: "prefer not to say" uses a neutral figure and average starting weights, and you can leave age, height and weight blank.

Voice coaching

Spoken rep counts and timer cues use the speech voices built into iOS, on your device. Nothing you do is recorded or sent anywhere. You can turn the voice off in Settings.

Purchases

LiftPulse Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; I never see your name, email address or payment details. The app only learns from Apple, on your device, whether you have Pro. Apple's own privacy policy covers your purchase.

What I collect

Nothing. LiftPulse has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or location tracking, and it does not use any third-party service that collects data. It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts or Apple Health.

Deleting your data

In LiftPulse, go to Settings > Erase all data, or delete the app.

Children

LiftPulse is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting data, this page will say so before it does.
